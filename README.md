GlassPane
A translucent notes panel that floats over your video call, so you can see the people you're meeting with while you type. When the meeting ends, it hands your raw notes to Claude and returns a recap with headings.
Built because notes taken in a normal notes app pile up unread, and because looking down to type means stopping looking at everyone else.
What it does
The panel sits on top of whatever else is on screen. Fade mode drops it to about a third opacity when untouched and brings it back the moment you reach for it, so it disappears while you're listening.
The editor has three heading levels, bold and italic, bulleted and numbered lists, checkboxes, and font sizes. Attendee chips drop @Name into the notes, which is shorthand while typing and how the model assigns owners to action items later.
Organize returns a structured recap: summary, sections, decisions, action items with owners and due dates, and open questions. Every note carries a filing status, and the title bar counts how many are still unfiled.
